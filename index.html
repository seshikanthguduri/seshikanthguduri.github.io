About

Discipline, automation, and a bias for reliability

I'm a Senior DevOps Platform & Site Reliability Engineer based in Phoenix, Arizona, currently working at Early Warning Services in Scottsdale. My work sits at the intersection of platform engineering, cloud architecture, and security — building the internal platforms that let product teams ship faster without breaking things.

Day to day, that means Kubernetes and AWS as the foundation, Terraform for repeatable infrastructure, GitOps with Argo CD for delivery, and SLOs/error budgets to keep reliability measurable instead of anecdotal. I care a lot about the boring, disciplined parts of the job: policy-as-code guardrails, audit-ready evidence, and automation that removes toil instead of adding another dashboard nobody reads.

Outside of my day job, I build public, from-scratch portfolio projects — a regulated-FinTech Kubernetes platform blueprint, a self-hosted LLM guardrail system, and an automated job-search pipeline — and I contribute to CNCF/Kubernetes documentation. I hold 16 industry certifications spanning Kubernetes/CNCF, AWS, and platform tooling — including CNCF Kubestronaut and AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional — and I'm currently working toward Istio Certified Associate.
